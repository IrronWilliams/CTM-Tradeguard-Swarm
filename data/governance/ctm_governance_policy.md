# Customer Trade Marketing (CTM) Corporate Governance, Checklist & Chart of Authority Policy

**Document ID:** `GOV-CTM-2026-V1`  
**Department:** Convenience Retail Trade Finance & CTM Accounting  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Section 1: Purpose & Corporate Accrual Accounting Policy

This document establishes the mandatory internal controls, audit verification rules, and approval authority for all Customer Trade Marketing (CTM) expense reimbursements and quarterly volume performance rebate payments across the National Convenience Retail portfolio.

### 1.1 Annual Accrual Elements Setup
At the beginning of each fiscal year, the CTM Accountant reviews each customer's annual CTM agreement to identify commercial sales plans, qualifying beverage packages, and element funding rates. Each contractual program is established in the CTM Accrual Ledger as a distinct **Element** tied to specific supporting beverage packages (physical cases shipped).

### 1.2 Quarterly Accrual Reviews & Rate True-Ups
At the close of each fiscal quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4), the CTM Accountant performs a formal **Quarterly Accrual Review**:
1. **ERP & Bottler Volume Reconciliation:** Forecasted volume is compared against actual physical cases shipped as reported in the ERP system and verified with regional bottling partners.
2. **Quarterly Accrual Rate Adjustments:** Based on actual YTD volume performance, tier attainment, and quarterly review discussions with Sales Executives and bottling partners, the CTM Accountant may hold the accrual rate steady or adjust the effective quarterly accrual rate per case up or down to reflect actual program liability and prevent material year-end over- or under-accruals.
3. **Customer Claim Settlement Rule:** While internal corporate accrual rates may be adjusted during quarterly reviews to manage general ledger liability, **customer rebate claims and vendor payments are strictly audited and paid according to the commercial terms, tiers, and rates specified in the customer's CTM agreement.**

## Section 2: Mandatory 6-Point CTM Payment Checklist

Every CTM payment request—whether submitted by an internal Sales Executive (travel, meeting, sponsorship, POS signage) or by a Retailer Corporate Accounting department (quarterly volume rebate, scan-down, or coupon settlement)—**must pass all six (6) items on the CTM Payment Checklist** prior to payment release:

1. **Checklist Item #1 — Valid Customer Contract on File:**
   * A valid CTM agreement must exist for the customer and fiscal period.
   * **Mandatory June 30 Unsigned Contract Rule:** If a customer's annual CTM agreement remains **UNSIGNED** after **June 30** of the current fiscal year (e.g., held up in retailer procurement/legal redlines), standard dollar-tier sign-offs are suspended. **ALL expenses and rebate payments for an unsigned customer contract after June 30 require mandatory sign-off by the Director of Trade Finance (CTM Director), regardless of dollar amount.**

2. **Checklist Item #2 — Assigned to Active Element with Sufficient Accrued Funds:**
   * The claim must reference a valid, active `element_id` belonging to that customer.
   * The requested payment amount must not exceed the element's current **Remaining Accrued Balance** (`YTD Accrued Budget - YTD Spend`).
   * **Upfront Advance Rule:** If an element includes a beginning-of-year Upfront Marketing Advance already paid in Q1, no additional mid-year payments may be released against that element unless cumulative YTD earned accruals exceed the upfront advance paid.

3. **Checklist Item #3 — Itemized Receipt / Proof of Performance (POP) Attached:**
   * **Sales Executive Expenses:** Must include an itemized third-party vendor invoice or itemized travel & entertainment (T&E) receipt. Credit card statement summaries without line-item detail are strictly rejected.
   * **Volume Rebates & Display Features:** Must include required Proof of Performance (POP) as mandated by the element (e.g., signed Field Merchandising Cold Vault Audit Report, bottler display compliance verification, or POS scan-down report).
   * **Coupon Elements:** Must include an official Third-Party Coupon Clearinghouse Redemption Report (e.g., Inmar or NCH) verifying actual consumer-redeemed coupons.

4. **Checklist Item #4 — Expense Type & Package Eligibility Under Element Terms:**
   * The expense activity, retail banner/region, and beverage packages claimed must be explicitly eligible under the specific contract element charged.
   * Prohibited items (such as retailer employee gift cards, capital store equipment/coolers, slotting fees, or excluded regional divisions/banners) must be rejected.

5. **Checklist Item #5 — Mathematical & ERP Volume Accuracy Verified:**
   * For volume-based rebates, claimed case volume must be reconciled against **ERP Actual Shipped Cases** for the exact supporting packages and fiscal quarter.
   * **Quarter-End Cutoff & Premature Claim Rule:** Quarterly performance rebates may only be settled **after** the fiscal quarter has officially closed and ERP shipments are finalized. Premature payment demands submitted prior to quarter-end close or based on estimated/projected volume must be rejected.
   * For tiered or YoY growth elements, the claimed rate per case must match the actual volume threshold or YoY growth percentage achieved in ERP.
   * For coupon elements, math must be calculated strictly on **actual redeemed coupons** (face value + contractual clearinghouse fee), never on total coupons printed or distributed.

6. **Checklist Item #6 — Signed Per Chart of Authority (COA):**
   * Once Checklist Items #1 through #5 are verified, the payment must be routed to the required approval level under the CTM Chart of Authority below.

## Section 3: CTM Chart of Authority (COA)

All approved CTM payments and volume rebate settlements require sign-off based on the verified payment amount and contract governance status:

| Approval Tier | Verified Payment Amount | Required Approver Role | Governance & Exception Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Up to \$2,500.00** | **CTM Accountant** | Eligible for direct CTM Accountant approval *only* if all 6 CTM Checklist items pass and the customer contract is signed. |
| **Tier 2** | **\$2,500.01 – \$50,000.00** | **CTM Manager** | Requires CTM Accountant audit verification + CTM Manager sign-off. |
| **Tier 3** | **\$50,000.01 – \$100,000.00** | **Director of Trade Finance** | Requires CTM Manager review + Director of Trade Finance sign-off. **Also mandatory for ANY dollar amount (even < \$2,500) if the customer's annual contract is UNSIGNED after June 30.** |
| **Tier 4** | **Over \$100,000.00** | **VP of Finance** | Executive sign-off required for all high-value national quarterly rebates exceeding \$100,000.00. |

## Section 1: Company History & Account Background

Founded in 1951 in El Paso, Texas (as Kay’s Food Stores) and acquired by Alimentation Couche-Tard in 2003, **Circle K** is one of the largest convenience and fuel retailers in North America, operating over **7,100 stores across 12 U.S. Business Units (Divisions)** spanning the Sunbelt, West Coast, Midwest, Great Lakes, Gulf Coast, and Southeast.

Circle K has been a cornerstone national CTM packaged beverage partner for over 30 years, requiring coordination across nearly every major U.S. Coca-Cola bottling partner. In fiscal year 2025, Circle K delivered 4,100,000 physical bottle/can cases. Due to Circle K’s decentralized regional business units and complex category management structure, the 2026 Joint Business Plan (JBP) is the most granular in the portfolio, structured across **seven (7) package-specific and volume-incentive CTM elements** (strictly bottle/can packaged beverages; fountain/syrup volume is excluded).

### 3. Element 403 — 20oz Sparkling YoY Volume Growth Incentive (`CK-20OZ-INC`)
* **Element ID:** `CK-20OZ-INC`
* **Supporting Package:** **20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET** only.
* **Tiered YoY Volume Growth Incentive Schedule (Retroactive to Case #1 per Quarter):**
  * **Below +3.0% YoY Quarterly Growth:** **\$0.00 per case** (No growth incentive earned).
  * **Tier 1 (+3.0% to +4.9% YoY Quarterly Growth):** **\$0.10 per case** on all 20oz Sparkling PET cases shipped in that fiscal quarter.
  * **Tier 2 ($\ge$ +5.0% YoY Quarterly Growth):** **\$0.15 per case** on all 20oz Sparkling PET cases shipped in that fiscal quarter.
* **2025 Prior-Year Quarterly Baselines (Contractual Benchmark):**
  * **2025-Q1 Baseline:** `270,000 cases`
  * **2025-Q2 Baseline:** `330,000 cases`
  * **2025-Q3 Baseline:** `350,000 cases`
  * **2025-Q4 Baseline:** `290,000 cases`
* **Reconciliation Rule:** YoY growth percentage is calculated strictly for the individual fiscal quarter (`(2026 Quarterly ERP Actual Cases - 2025 Quarterly Baseline Cases) / 2025 Quarterly Baseline Cases`), not cumulative YTD growth.

## Section 1: Company History & Account Background

Founded in 1803 in New Jersey and entering the dairy business in 1902 in Wawa, Pennsylvania before opening its first Wawa Food Market in 1964, **Wawa, Inc.** operates over **1,060 large-format, high-volume company-owned convenience stores** across the Mid-Atlantic (PA, NJ, DE, MD, VA, DC), Florida, and expanding Southeast markets.

Wawa is one of the highest-velocity single-serve and foodservice partners in the national convenience portfolio, delivering 1,920,000 physical bottle/can cases in 2025. Wawa’s category management team is known as the most demanding negotiating counterparty in the convenience retail portfolio—frequently holding annual CTM agreements in legal/procurement redlines past mid-year while demanding upfront annual advances and aggressive multi-tier volume settlements. Regional bottling distribution is led by Liberty Coca-Cola Beverages (Mid-Atlantic) and Coca-Cola Beverages Florida.

## Section 1: Company History & Account Background

Founded in 1933 by Leon Hess in New Jersey, **Hess Corporation** is one of the premier convenience and fuel retailers along the U.S. East Coast, operating **1,250 company-operated Hess Express** locations from Massachusetts to Florida. Hess has been a strategic national CTM partner for over 18 years, known for high-velocity forecourt traffic, strong cold-vault execution, and annual summer promotional campaigns aligned with their signature seasonal retail promotions.

In fiscal year 2025, Hess delivered 680,000 total physical cases across core sparkling and still beverage packages. For 2026, the Joint Business Plan (JBP) targets **720,000 total physical cases** across four (4) package-supported CTM accrual elements (strictly bottle/can packaged beverages; fountain excluded).

## Section 2: Accrual Elements, Supporting Packages & Commercial Terms

### 1. Element 101 — General Promotions & Customer Development (`HESS-PROMO`)
* **Element ID:** `HESS-PROMO`
* **Supporting Packages:** **20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET** and **1-Liter Core Sparkling PET**.
* **Contract Base Accrual Rate:** **\$0.15** per physical case shipped of supporting packages.
* **Eligible Expenses:**
  * Joint Business Planning (JBP) customer meetings and catering
  * Regional convenience trade show booth fees
  * Authorized Sales Executive customer travel and entertainment (T&E)
  * Point-of-Sale (POS) cooler door decals and static clings
* **Ineligible Expenses:**
  * Retailer employee or store manager gift cards
  * Store capital equipment or permanent coolers
  * Slotting fees or any expense submitted without an itemized third-party receipt/invoice
* **Documentation Required:** Itemized third-party vendor invoice or itemized T&E receipt (credit card statements alone are insufficient).

### 2. Element 102 — 20oz Single-Serve Cold Vault Rebate (`HESS-20OZ`)
* **Element ID:** `HESS-20OZ`
* **Supporting Package:** **20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET** only.
* **Contract Base Accrual / Rebate Rate:** **\$0.40** per physical case shipped.
* **Payout Structure:** Pure volume-based rebate paid quarterly upon reconciliation of QTD ERP actual shipped case volume of 20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET.
* **Performance Requirement:** Hess must maintain Top-2 shelf placement (eye-level) in the main cold vault door across at least 90% of participating Hess Express stores.
* **Documentation Required:** Quarterly Customer Rebate Claim Form + signed Field Merchandising Cold Vault Audit Report verifying $\ge$ 90% compliance (Proof of Performance).

### 3. Element 103 — Summer "Pump & Pour" Endcap Feature (`HESS-SUMMER`)
* **Element ID:** `HESS-SUMMER`
* **Supporting Package:** **12-Pack 12oz Cans** shipped during **Q2 and Q3 only** (April 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026).
* **Contract Base Accrual Rate:** **\$0.25** per physical case shipped during Q2 and Q3.
* **Eligible Expenses:** Promotional retail price-off funding and front-lobby endcap display allowances supporting the Memorial Day through Labor Day "Pump & Pour" campaign.
* **Ineligible Expenses:** Non-summer (Q1/Q4) promotions, store manager sales contest gift cards, or non-12-pack packages.
* **Documentation Required:** Proof of Performance (bottler store display compliance report or promotional circular ad tear-sheet) + calculation worksheet.

### 4. Element 104 — Fuel-Pump Receipt & In-Store Tear-Pad Coupon Program (`HESS-COUPON`)
* **Element ID:** `HESS-COUPON`
* **Supporting Packages:** **20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET** and **12-Pack 12oz Cans**.
* **Contract Base Accrual Rate:** **\$0.12** per physical case shipped.
* **Eligible Expenses & Reimbursement Rate:**
  * Reimburses **actual consumer-redeemed coupons** at **\$1.00 face value** (valid on the purchase of two 20oz bottles or one 12-pack) **plus a \$0.08 per-coupon clearinghouse handling fee** (**\$1.08 total reimbursement per verified redeemed coupon**).
* **Strict Reconciliation & Documentation Rule:**
  * Reimbursements are paid **strictly on actual consumer-redeemed coupons** verified by an official **Third-Party Coupon Clearinghouse Redemption Summary (Inmar or NCH)**.
  * Unredeemed coupons, paper printing costs, or claims calculated on total coupons *printed* or *distributed* at the fuel pumps are **strictly ineligible**.

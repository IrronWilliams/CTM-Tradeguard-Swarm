## Section 1: Company History & Account Background

Tracing its roots to 1909 and expanding its U.S. convenience retail footprint through major acquisitions including Amoco and ARCO, **BP North America** operates and franchises over **2,900 premier convenience retail sites** across two primary retail banners: **BP Connect / Thorntons** (Midwest & East Coast) and **ampm** (West Coast & Southwest).

BP has been a flagship national CTM account for over 25 years, characterized by multi-region bottler coordination across six (6) major regional bottling territories and high-volume multi-pack warm display promotions. In fiscal year 2025, BP delivered 1,450,000 physical bottle/can cases. The 2026 Joint Business Plan (JBP) governs **four (4) CTM accrual elements** across packaged beverages (fountain excluded).

## Section 2: Accrual Elements, Supporting Packages & Commercial Terms

### 1. Element 201 — General Promotions & National Account Development (`BP-PROMO`)
* **Element ID:** `BP-PROMO`
* **Supporting Packages:** **20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET**, **20oz Sports Drinks PET**, and **16oz Energy Cans** across both *BP Connect* and *ampm* banners.
* **Contract Base Accrual Rate:** **\$0.10** per physical case shipped.
* **Eligible Expenses:**
  * National Franchisee Convention sponsorships and booth displays
  * Sales Executive travel and customer business review meetings
  * Regional bottler alignment meetings
  * POS pump-topper and window signage
* **Ineligible Expenses:** Capital equipment, franchise owner cash allowances, personal travel extensions, or any expense submitted without itemized third-party receipts.
* **Documentation Required:** Itemized third-party vendor invoice or itemized T&E receipt.

---

### 2. Element 202 — 12-Pack Case Pack Beverage Tiered Rebate (`BP-12PK`)
* **Element ID:** `BP-12PK`
* **Supporting Package:** **12-Pack 12oz Cans (Core Sparkling Brands)**.
* **Tiered Quarterly Rebate Schedule (Retroactive to Case #1 per Quarter):**
  * **Tier 1 (Base Rate):** **\$0.30 per physical case** when quarterly shipped volume is between **1 and 99,999 cases**.
  * **Tier 2 (Stretch Tier):** **\$0.35 per physical case** (retroactive to case #1 for that quarter) *only if* quarterly ERP shipped volume reaches or exceeds **100,000 physical cases** within that specific fiscal quarter.
* **Reconciliation Rule:** Each quarter stands alone. Achieving Tier 2 (\$0.35/case) in one quarter (e.g., Q2) does **not** entitle BP to the \$0.35 rate in a subsequent quarter (e.g., Q3) if that subsequent quarter's ERP shipped volume falls below 100,000 cases.
* **Performance Requirement:** Mandatory front-lobby 20-case warm stack display during major holiday periods; requires quarterly ERP volume verification + signed Bottler Display Compliance Report.

### 3. Element 203 — *ampm* West Coast Foodservice Combo Promotion (`BP-COMBO`)
* **Element ID:** `BP-COMBO`
* **Supporting Packages:** **20oz Single-Serve Sparkling PET** and **20oz Dasani Water PET** shipped specifically to West Coast **ampm** locations.
* **Contract Base Accrual Rate:** **\$0.20** per physical case shipped.
* **Eligible Expenses:** Register scan-down funding and digital menu board media for the "\$2.99 Hot Dog/Taquito + 20oz Beverage" combo promotion at *ampm* stores.
* **Strict Banner / Regional Exclusion:** Midwest and East Coast **BP Connect / Thorntons** stores are **strictly excluded** from `BP-COMBO`. Any promotional claims or expenses from *BP Connect* locations charged to `BP-COMBO` must be rejected.
* **Documentation Required:** West Coast *ampm* POS register combo scan-down report or approved *ampm* marketing invoice.

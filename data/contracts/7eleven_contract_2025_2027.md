## Section 1: Company History & Account Background

Founded in 1927 in Dallas, Texas as the Southland Ice Company (operating as Tote’m Stores before being renamed 7-Eleven in 1946 to reflect its extended hours of operation), **7-Eleven, Inc.** is the largest convenience retailer in the world, operating and franchising over **13,000 U.S. stores** across the *7-Eleven*, *Speedway*, and *Stripes* retail banners.

Headquartered in Irving, Texas, 7-Eleven is the single largest packaged beverage account in the national CTM portfolio, delivering 5,200,000 physical bottle/can cases in fiscal year 2025. Operating under a **3-Year Multi-Year Master CTM Agreement (2025–2027)**, 7-Eleven’s trade marketing program is heavily structured around **strictly audited store-level performance elements**.

---

## Section 2: Strict Quarterly Settlement Timing & Anti-Premature Payment Policy

Because 7-Eleven’s corporate accounting department frequently submits invoices demanding quarterly performance payments 2 to 3 weeks **prior** to the end of the fiscal quarter based on projected case volume:

1. **No Premature Quarterly Settlements:** Quarterly performance rebates and allowances under this Multi-Year Master Agreement may **ONLY** be calculated, audited, and paid **after the fiscal quarter has officially ended** (March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31).
2. **Mandatory Post-Close Reconciliation:** Payment requires both:
   * Final closed quarter-end **ERP Actual Shipped Cases** reported by all U.S. bottling partners for that completed quarter, **and**
   * Final completed **Third-Party Store Merchandising & Planogram Audit Scores** for that completed quarter.
3. **Rejection of Estimated/Advance Claims:** Any claim submitted prior to the end of the fiscal quarter, or calculated using estimated/projected quarterly case volume, must be **immediately rejected** under CTM Checklist Items #2, #3, and #5.
